# Roadmap: remaining OPERA S&E capabilities

Built: bookings and statuses, function diary with conflict control, events, revenue and cost lines,
payments, commission and shares, activities, CRM, dashboards, Excel import and export, multi-tenant SaaS core.

Next, in suggested order:

1. **Proposals & contracts.** Branded PDF quote or contract from a booking, e-signature, and an automatic DEF on signature.
2. **Packages & menus catalogue.** Reusable priced packages (per-pax menus, décor sets) to add to bookings in one click; price lists per season.
3. **Banquet Event Orders (BEO).** Per-event operational sheet (timings, menu, setup, AV, staffing) for operations and the venue.
4. **Deposits & payment schedules.** Due dates, reminders, invoice numbering, receipts; integration with accounting (Zoho/Odoo/QuickBooks).
5. **Room blocks.** Accommodation blocks and pickup for weddings and conferences.
6. **Budgets & forecasts.** Monthly targets per AM and business unit, compared with actuals and pipeline.
7. **Communication.** WhatsApp / email templates and logging from activities; Instagram lead capture.
8. **Subscription billing.** Stripe (or a regional payment gateway) checkout and plan enforcement; admin console for the SaaS operator.
9. **Arabic UI and RTL**, and localisation of dates and numbers.
10. **Audit log and SSO** (Google / Microsoft) for enterprise tenants.
